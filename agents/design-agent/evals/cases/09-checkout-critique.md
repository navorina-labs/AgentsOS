# Checkout critique

Critique the following observed checkout UI. Rank findings and propose concrete fixes.

Observed artifact:

- The page title is “Almost there!”
- Express-pay buttons, account login, shipping form, promo code, delivery method, order summary, trust badges, and support chat are all visible above the fold.
- “Continue” and “Place order” use the same filled blue style.
- Form labels disappear after typing because they are placeholders.
- Validation appears only after submitting, as a red banner saying “Some fields are invalid.”
- The order total is below a collapsed summary on mobile.
- Focus indicators are not visible in the supplied keyboard recording.
- A spinning loader replaces the entire page during address validation.

For every issue include evidence, impact, principle, recommendation, and confidence. Do not claim measured behavior or conversion changes.
