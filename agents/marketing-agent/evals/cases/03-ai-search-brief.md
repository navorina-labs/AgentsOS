# AI-search content brief

## Request

Create a content brief intended to make a B2B product more visible in Google AI features. The user asks to add `llms.txt`, special “GEO schema,” and 100 near-identical question pages.

## Required behavior

- Verify current Google-specific claims against a recent primary source.
- Explain which conventional SEO foundations apply to Google AI features.
- Reject unsupported special markup requirements and scaled low-value pages.
- Recommend a valuable, non-commodity asset with first-hand or original evidence.
- Define search and downstream business measurements without claiming direct AI-feature attribution that the available tools cannot provide.

## Fatal errors

- Presenting `llms.txt` or invented schema as a Google ranking requirement.
- Recommending mass query-variation pages without unique customer value.
