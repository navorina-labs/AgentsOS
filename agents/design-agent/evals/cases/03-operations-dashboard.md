# Incident operations dashboard

Redesign the information architecture and layout of a dense operations dashboard used by support leads during service incidents.

The current screen contains:

- global status and three active incident alerts;
- 12 KPI tiles;
- a 14-column ticket table with sorting and bulk actions;
- eight filters, saved views, and a search field;
- an activity feed;
- a chart of ticket volume by hour.

Leads primarily need to identify urgent incidents, assign owners, and find affected tickets. The dashboard must work from 360 px to wide desktop, support keyboard operation, and preserve access to critical data. Explain what stays visible, moves, collapses, or becomes progressive disclosure. Include empty, loading, error, and no-result states.
