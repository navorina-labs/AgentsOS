# Product launch presentation

Design the narrative and composition system for a 10-slide product launch deck introducing a collaborative AI workspace to customers.

Available evidence:

- The product combines documents, conversations, and tasks in one project space.
- A live demo can show creating a brief, assigning a task, and tracing an AI answer to a source.
- No approved customer quotes or launch metrics are available.
- The deck will be presented on a projector, shared as a PDF, and sometimes read on a phone.

Provide a slide-by-slide story, content hierarchy, visual system, motion guidance for the live version, responsive/readability considerations, accessibility, and an evidence policy. Do not fabricate proof.
